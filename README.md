# RB-Avionics cases

3D-printable cases and mounts, and aluminium boxes, for
[RB-Avionics](https://www.rbavionics.com): open-source, open-design avionics for
experimental and ultralight aircraft and for flight simulator cockpits.

The printed designs are also published on Thingiverse:
[XIAPROJECTS designs](https://www.thingiverse.com/XIAPROJECTS/designs).

## KiCad board templates

Every design has a KiCad 9 project in its `kicad/` folder: a board outline that
fits the case, mounting holes on the case's own bosses, and the case cut at the
board plane on the User.Drawings layer. Open it in KiCad and add your circuit;
each design README shows the board and its notes. The cockpits get a backplane
that sits behind the panel. The templates are generated by
[tools/kicad_templates.py](tools/kicad_templates.py) from the case STLs.

## Printing for aircraft

The cases are not certified aircraft parts. Printed parts installed in an
aircraft must withstand 100 °C in cruise on long flights: PLA, PETG, ABS and
ASA do not. Before printing, read [PRINTING.md](PRINTING.md) for the
materials, the print requirements and the oven test.

## Licenses

RB-Avionics designs are free for personal use; commercial use requires an
agreement with RB-Avionics. The full terms are in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

A design by another author keeps its own license in a `LICENSE.txt` in its
folder: the [GPS mount V2](RB-02/gps-mount-v2-7169108/) is by SeeCoryRun, under
CC BY-SA.

## Repository layout

```
RB-xx/                     one folder per product
  <design>-<thing id>/     a design published on Thingiverse
    README.md              description, parts, photos, author and license
    LICENSE.txt            only for designs by other authors
    files/                 STL files to print, DXF files to cut
    images/                renders and photos
    kicad/<project>/       KiCad board template for your own electronics
  <design>/                a design not on Thingiverse: same layout
RB-Cockpits/               simulator panels generated by tools/cockpit_panels.py
LICENSE.md                 license of the RB-Avionics designs
PRINTING.md                material requirements for parts that fly
tools/                     import, check and generator scripts
```
